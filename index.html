Free Online Image Compressor

Compress My Image helps you reduce JPG, PNG and WebP file sizes directly in your browser. The tool is designed for websites, social media, email and everyday file sharing.

How it works

  1. Choose an image from your device.
  2. Select the compression quality.
  3. Click Compress Image.
  4. Download your smaller image.

Privacy

Your images are processed locally in your browser. We do not require an account and the tool does not need to upload your images to our server for compression.

Supported Formats

JPG, JPEG, PNG and WebP images are supported.
