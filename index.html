Free Online Bulk Image Compressor

Compress My Image helps you reduce JPG, PNG and WebP file sizes in bulk directly in your browser.

How it works

  1. Select multiple images from your device.
  2. Adjust the compression quality slider.
  3. Click "Compress Images" to process them all at once.
  4. Download them individually or all together.

Privacy

Your images are processed locally in your browser. No files are uploaded to any server.
